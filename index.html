Skill moves

Every skill in the game. Keyboard key, then the iPad button.

ShootT · SHOOT

Kick at the goal.

Curve shotHold W/S + T · joystick up or down + SHOOT

The ball bends into the corner.

PassR · PASS

Pass to your most open teammate.

CrossQ · CROSS

A high ball into the box.

Rainbow flickF · FLICK

Flip the ball over your head and the defender, then run onto it.

HeaderH · HEADER (or SHOOT when the ball drops next to you)

Jump and head a cross into the goal!

Bicycle kickB · BICYCLE

Flying upside-down kick. Best after a cross!

Scissor kickV · SCISSOR

Flying side kick. Best after a cross!

Slide tackleE · TACKLE

Slide to win the ball. Miss it and it's a foul!

InjuriesHappen by themselves

A hard foul makes a CRACK! Sometimes the player gets hurt and limps (slower) for a while. 🩹

SprintShift · SPRINT

Run faster. Watch your energy bar.

Switch playerSpace · SWITCH

Control the teammate closest to the ball.

RunArrows or WASD · joystick

Move your player around the field.

Penalty shotA/W/D then T · joystick then SHOOT

Aim left, middle or right, then shoot.

Goalie diveA/W/D · joystick

In penalties, dive to save the shot.

Skip replaySpace · SWITCH or SHOOT

Skip the goal replay.

Change cameraC

Close, TV or whole-field camera.

PauseP

Take a break (not in online games).

Fouls

A slide tackle that misses the ball is a foul. The referee might show a yellow card. Two yellows make a red, and that player is sent off!

A tie?

Extra time first. Still tied? Penalty shootout!

Aim with A W D or ← ↑ →, shoot with T. Your goalie dives with the same keys.
